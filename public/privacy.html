Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Gatho collects as little as possible. Guests don't need an account, and we don't ask guests for an email address or phone number.

What we collect

  • Hosts: your email address and the events you create (title, time, location, details and any cover photo).
  • Guests: the name you type, your reply (going, maybe or can't go), any +1s, and any comments you post.
  • On your device: a random ID and your last-used name are saved in your browser so you can come back and change your reply. This is not used to track you across other sites.

Who can see it

  • Anyone with an event link can see that event's details, the going and maybe counts, and its comments (with the names of the people who posted them).
  • Only the host can see the full list of names and replies.
  • Event pages ask search engines not to list them.

Cover photos

Photos are resized and re-saved before upload, which removes hidden data such as GPS location. Anyone with the event link can see the cover photo, so please don't upload anything private.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with our hosting providers (Supabase for the database and photos, Cloudflare for the website). We don't sell your data or show ads.

Deleting your data

  • Hosts can delete an event at any time, which also deletes its replies, comments and cover photo.
  • Hosts can remove any reply or comment on their events.
  • To delete your host account or have a guest reply removed, contact us at the address below.
